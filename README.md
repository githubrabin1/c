✅ SET–A (FM:10)
1. What is YerelNet?

YerelNet is a web portal and web-enabled communication platform for local governments in Turkey. It allows sharing of data, information, and communication among local government officials, academics, and citizens.

2. What is the application of YerelNet?

The application of YerelNet is to provide an online forum where Turkish local governments can:

Share data

Provide information about themselves

Communicate and collaborate

Build knowledge and promote transparency

3. Who are the stakeholders of YerelNet?

Local governments of Turkey

Academics and researchers

Private firms working with local government

Citizens

Turkish State Planning Organization (supporting organization)

4. What are the benefits of YerelNet?

Reduces coordination and information dissemination costs

Encourages transparency and accountability

Supports sharing of best practices and standardization

Increases citizen involvement

Supports collective action among local governments

✅ SET–B (FM:10)
1. What is the case study about?

The case study is about Village Information Kiosks used by the Warana Group of Cooperatives in India to support sugar cane production through digitalized services.

2. What is the key focus area of this case?

The focus is on using village kiosks and ICT to improve:

Registration for plantation

Issuing harvesting permits

Providing payment information
…thus improving the overall sugar cane production process.

3. Who are the stakeholders of this case?

Farmers

Warana Group of Cooperatives (WGC)

Village Information Kiosk operators

Local ISP (for internet services)

4. What are the cost benefits of this case?

Saves farmers significant time (from days to minutes)

Faster access to income and expenditure information

Better fertilizer stock management

Approx. US$750,000 saved for the cooperative

Project cost: US$600,000 (benefits exceed cost)

✅ SET–C (FM:10)
1. What do you mean by BRIS and RCC?

BRIS: Birth Registration Information System (Electronic birth registration system)

RCC: Rajshahi City Corporation (local government authority in Bangladesh)

2. Which languages does BRIS support?

BRIS supports:

Bengali

English (for certificates and reports)

3. Who are the stakeholders of this case?

Rajshahi City Corporation (RCC)

Health agencies

Statistics agencies

Other potential stakeholders: immigration, elections, education, and local government departments

4. What are the cost benefits of this case?

Removes duplication of records

Reduces error rates

Faster processing, searching, reporting

Increases registration and immunization rates

Very low cost:

Development cost: < US$20,000

Monthly operational cost: ~US$200
